    How the ranking works

    1Where today sits

    Every weekday exchange rate in the period is compared with today's. The share of days today beats is the FX percentile.

    2Adjust for inflation

    Past rates are restated in today's prices using inflation at home and in the destination, so a currency that fell only because prices soared isn't mistaken for a bargain.

    3Score

    The Quantile Score is half how rare today's inflation-adjusted rate is, half how large its advantage over the period average is.

    Rates are mid-market reference rates; banks and cards add a margin. Inflation covers whole economies, not just tourist prices. Quantile describes the past and makes no forecast.
